% ===== VARIANT 1 (main) =====

\paragraph{Recall separates the models, precision does not.}
Across the filled rows of Table~\ref{tab:grounding-results}, document-level
recall spans 0.583 to 0.735 with non-overlapping confidence intervals,
whereas label-verified precision stays between 0.530 and 0.536 and every
interval overlaps. Each row changes only the model acting as extractor and
disambiguation judge, and the Wikidata grounding stage is deterministic and
shared. Recall therefore reflects the coverage the model contributes, and
the precision lower bound behaves as a property of the pipeline rather than
of the model. The three pending open-weight rows will show whether this
pattern holds beyond cloud models.

\paragraph{Recall does not track general model capability.}
GPT-5.4, the strongest generalist in the lineup, records the lowest recall
in Table~\ref{tab:grounding-results} at 0.583, trailing DeepSeek-V4-Flash by
0.074 and Gemini-3.1-Flash-Lite by 0.152. Its figure is a strict lower bound
over 99 of 100 articles, but the missing article accounts for at most 347 of
the 7174 gold mentions, so even perfect recovery there would add at most
0.048 and leave the ordering intact. A cautious reading is that extraction
in this pipeline rewards exhaustive enumeration of candidate surfaces rather
than reasoning depth. We do not draw a stronger conclusion from three rows.
