\textit{Wikipedia} is our corpus of Russian Wikipedia articles on ancient
history. It comprises 100 articles drawn from ten fixed thematic sections:
Sumer and Mesopotamia, Ancient Egypt, Assyria, the Hittite Kingdom, Phoenicia,
Achaemenid Iran, Ancient India, Ancient China, Ancient Greece, and Ancient
Rome. Each section is defined by a root category in the Russian Wikipedia
category graph, and its candidate pool consists of all article pages in the
corresponding category subtree. A candidate article is accepted if its body
paragraphs contain at least 30 unique links to other articles, which serves as
a proxy for substantive encyclopedic content, and if the earliest date recorded
for it in Wikidata precedes 500~CE.\footnote{We consider the Wikidata
properties inception (P571), start time (P580), point in time (P585), date of
birth (P569), and publication date (P577). Articles with no recorded date are
retained.} Articles typed in Wikidata as films, paintings, or museums are
excluded. Candidates within each section are shuffled with a fixed random
seed, and the first ten articles that satisfy these criteria are selected,
which makes the corpus construction deterministic and reproducible. Since the
Wikipedia category structure is noisy, a small number of retained undated
articles fall outside the target period. We keep them to avoid manual
curation. The article HTML was collected in July 2026. We remove navigation
elements, infoboxes, tables, and reference markup, and extract body
paragraphs. After removal of empty segments, the corpus contains 2553
paragraphs and 160836 words of Russian source text, with an average paragraph
length of 63 words. The same articles are also used in the terminology
recognition and grounding evaluation (Section~\ref{sec:results-terminology}).
