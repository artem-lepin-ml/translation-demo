\caption{LLM-as-a-judge comparison on BOUQuET (ru$\rightarrow$en).
Every judge re-scores the same four fixed translation systems (TranslateGemma,
Qwen3.6-27B, and their refined variants) over 198 paragraphs on three 1--10
criteria (accuracy, fluency, style), for $198 \times 4 \times 3 = 2\,376$ judge
calls per judge. Translations are never regenerated, so all differences are
attributable to the judge. All judges use the identical \texttt{v1\_core3}
prompt set. Local judges run at $T{=}0$ with thinking off. Frontier judges
(Claude Opus 4.8, GPT-5.5, Gemini-3.1-Flash-Lite) run at provider-default
effort, with the exact per-judge reasoning configuration disclosed in
Appendix~\ref{app:judge-configs}. Mean scores are averaged over the four
systems. $\rho$ is the Spearman correlation between per-paragraph judge
scores and automatic metrics: MetricX-24 (reference-based, lower is better,
so a negative $\rho$ indicates agreement) and CometKiwi (reference-free,
higher is better). Ties is the share of scores in $\{9,10\}$.
Per-system score breakdowns and exact judge configurations are listed in
Appendix~\ref{app:judge-configs}.}
