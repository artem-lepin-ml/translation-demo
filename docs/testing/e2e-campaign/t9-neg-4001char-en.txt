Paragraph 1. During the reign of Sargon of Akkad the city of Akkad remained an important center of southern Mesopotamia. The scribes of Akkad kept accounts of grain, livestock and silver, while the temple estates traded with Lagash. The year-names of this ruler mention the construction of canals and defensive walls.
