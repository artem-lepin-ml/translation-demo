=== TRANSLATION (English) ===
Babylon was one of the largest cities of ancient Mesopotamia, located on the banks of the Euphrates.

In the 18th century BC, the mayor of Babylon, Hammurapi, issued a famous code of laws carved on a basalt stele.

After Hammurabi's death the kingdom weakened, and the city later changed hands more than once.
